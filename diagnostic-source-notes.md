# Diagnostic room research notes

Purpose: concise source-backed technical explainers and patient checklists for the DiagnoGuide room panels. The public-facing checklist wording stays general and directs every patient to their own facility/clinician for instructions; it does not tell patients to fast, change fluids, stop medicines, or make contrast/implant/pregnancy decisions. No result interpretation or diagnosis is provided.

## Direct-page validation completed
- RadiologyInfo opened directly for breast tomosynthesis, pelvis ultrasound, breast ultrasound, breast biopsy, and DEXA.
- CDC opened directly for cervical screening; the page explains sample collection, Pap cytology, HPV testing and the importance of facility guidance.
- RadiologyInfo opened for abdominal/pelvic MRI (page shell confirmed); ISUOG opened for pregnancy ultrasound.
- The Society for Endocrinology page returned article text about immunoassay and tandem mass spectrometry but also triggered an access challenge in the browser; use only general, non-numeric method wording and preserve this limitation.

## Patient checklist guardrail
Each room checklist has five questions/checks that prompt the patient to confirm appointment-specific preparation, medicines, relevant pregnancy/device/contrast concerns where applicable, procedure/comfort/privacy, and results/aftercare with their own care team. They are prompts, not treatment instructions.
